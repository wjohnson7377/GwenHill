Before you apply

A little preparation can save a second trip and help you compare programs clearly.

01
Open the official source

Use the resource link above and review current dates, eligibility and format directly with the provider.

02
Ask what “free” covers

Confirm whether tuition, books, exams, equipment and transportation are included—or if any separate costs remain.

03
Keep your own notes

Write down who you spoke with, the date, what they need from you and the next deadline.

Programs can change funding, dates and eligibility. Verify details directly with each organization before applying. For an emergency, call 9-1-1.
